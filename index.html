Quick troubleshooting

No ESP32 connection
Reconnect to ESP32 Wi-Fi or your configured router, then reload.

DHT11 sensor error
Check supply, GND and DATA on GPIO 4.

Pump not operating
Check external pump supply, driver and relay polarity. Test with pump power disconnected first.

RPM remains zero
Check Hall wiring, magnet alignment and one-pulse-per-revolution assumption.

Settings & Wiring

ESP32 DevKit V1 configuration

Pin configuration

Hall sensor: GPIO 27

DHT11 data: GPIO 4

New pump relay: GPIO 25

Old pump relay: GPIO 26

Hall magnet count: 1 magnet / revolution

Relay polarity safety

Firmware currently defaults to Active LOW, matching the supplied Arduino sketch. Your relay type is not yet confirmed.

Do not connect pump power until you verify relay polarity. Test with the pumps disconnected and confirm both relays remain OFF at boot and when the ESP32 is reset.

To change polarity, edit RELAY_ACTIVE_LOW in src/main.cpp and upload firmware again.

Weather service

Weather data is fetched by the browser from Open-Meteo. Internet access is required for city lookup and forecast. Local ESP32 sensor monitoring and pump control continue without weather internet.

Prototype dashboard · ESP32 local web server · Weather values are forecasts, not DHT11 measurements. Keep emergency stop accessible and use a properly rated driver/relay for the pump load.
